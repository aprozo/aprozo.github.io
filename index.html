About

I am an experimental particle physicist working at the intersection of heavy-ion physics, detector science, and AI-assisted analysis.

My current research lives inside the STAR collaboration at RHIC — D0-tagged and inclusive jet measurements, AI-based unfolding with OmniFold — and within ePIC at the future Electron-Ion Collider, where I work on the backward hadronic calorimeter and user-learning programs. Earlier, I spent six years inside HADES at GSI/FAIR, with the electromagnetic calorimeter as my home: calibration, assembly, operations, and a doctoral thesis on neutral-meson production in Ag+Ag at 1.58 A GeV.

Outside the wire, I build small AI tools and bots — most visibly SciBot, an AI assistant for long-term access to RHIC knowledge — and I help organize workshops and onboarding for early-career scientists.
