I keep promising myself I'll write more, so here we go. This is a quiet corner of the internet where I'll
post short things: ideas from work, half-finished projects, occasional travel notes from beam-time trips,
and the very occasional rant about ROOT macros that won't compile.

## Why a blog

A few reasons:

- **External memory.** I forget what I figured out two weeks ago. Writing it down forces me to actually
  understand it, and means I can re-read it later.
- **Practice.** Talks, papers, and proposals are all the same skill — turning fuzzy thoughts into clear
  sentences. The more I do it in low-stakes form, the easier it gets at high stakes.
- **Public notes.** If anything here saves another junior physicist an afternoon, it was worth writing.

## What to expect

Three rough buckets:

1. **Physics** — short, intuition-first notes about heavy-ion physics, jets, and calorimetry.
2. **Projects** — what I'm building, mostly software, sometimes hardware. SciBot, small RAG experiments,
   ROOT/Python tricks, ePIC simulation work.
3. **Diary** — the human side. Where I am, what I'm reading, what conferences I just survived.

No schedule. No theme. Comments off. RSS maybe later.

> "Write the post you wish you'd found six months ago."

That's the bar.
